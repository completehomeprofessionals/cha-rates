Lawrence figures cover the Lawrence metro (Douglas County) as reported by the Lawrence Board of REALTORS®. Kansas City sales, volume and price figures include all closed transactions in the Heartland MLS (Kansas and Missouri); KC permits and appreciation are for the Kansas City, KS-MO metro. Appreciation is the year-over-year change in the FHFA all-transactions house price index (4th quarter). Permits are single-family building permits. History comes from past WSU forecast editions: 2022–2024 from the 2026 edition, 2021 from the 2025 edition, 2019–2020 from the 2024 edition, and 2018 from the 2023 edition. Each year uses the newest edition that reports it as final. WSU revises past figures slightly between editions (and changed its Lawrence data source from the Lawrence MLS to the Lawrence Board of REALTORS® in the 2026 edition), so year-over-year changes that cross editions are approximate. Where WSU published a year-over-year change, the dashboard shows WSU's figure.

Forecasts are projections, not guarantees. Complete Home Advisors shares this information for general education; it is not financial advice.
