Know your options

Which loan fits?

Conventional

Not backed by the government; the most common loan
  • As little as 3% down for some first-time buyers. 20% down avoids private mortgage insurance (PMI).
  • PMI can be removed once you reach enough equity.
  • Usually best for buyers with strong credit and a larger down payment.

FHA

Insured by the Federal Housing Administration
  • As little as 3.5% down with a qualifying credit score.
  • More flexible credit guidelines than conventional loans.
  • Comes with an upfront and an annual mortgage insurance premium.

VA

Guaranteed by the Department of Veterans Affairs
  • For eligible veterans, active-duty service members and some surviving spouses.
  • No down payment required and no monthly mortgage insurance.
  • A one-time funding fee applies (some borrowers are exempt).

USDA

Guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Agriculture
  • No down payment required for homes in USDA-eligible rural and suburban areas.
  • Household income limits apply.
  • Comes with an upfront and an annual guarantee fee.
