How to use these numbers

Trends, not exact values

This data covers homes listed for sale (asking prices, listing counts, time on market), not closed sales. It's best for comparing direction: is inventory rising faster here than nationally? Are prices cooling sooner? For local sold prices, see our monthly MLS report.

Why compare to the U.S.?

National headlines often don't match what's happening here. Comparing side by side shows whether our markets are hotter, cooler or simply following the national pattern, which helps with pricing and timing decisions.

Source: Realtor.com® Economic Research monthly housing inventory data, retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Updated monthly.

Lawrence = Lawrence, KS metro area (Douglas County). Kansas City = Kansas City metro area, Kansas and Missouri. Johnson County = Johnson County, KS. Includes single-family homes and condos/townhomes listed for sale. These figures differ from MLS sold-home statistics because they measure listings, not closings.
